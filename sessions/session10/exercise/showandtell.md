# Session 10 Exercise
## Show and Tell: App Progress + Exam Readiness

Following some of your wishes from Session 8, this exercise helps you learn from each other by presenting your app work-in-progress and getting targeted feedback before the exam.

## Learning goals

By the end of this activity, you should be able to:

1. Explain your app idea clearly and confidently.
2. Describe how your design decisions support your concept.
3. Reflect on coding challenges and share practical solutions.
4. Identify what is still missing for exam readiness.

## Before you start (5-10 min prep)

Each group prepares:

1. An explanation of the concept
2. The Figma designs
3. The working code/demo of the app

## What each group must cover in the presentation

### 1. Concept: how it came about

Explain:

1. What problem or need your app addresses
2. Where the idea came from (personal experience, observation, inspiration, etc.).
3. Who the main user is.
4. What the most important user action is.

### 2. Design: how the concept became visual

Explain:

1. How your concept turned into a visual identity.
2. Your choices for color, typography, spacing, and overall style.
3. How your UI supports usability and accessibility.
4. One design change you made after testing or feedback.

### 3. Coding: what was hardest

Explain:

1. The hardest technical part so far.
2. Why it was difficult.
3. How you solved it, or what your current strategy is.
4. What is still the hardest part right now.


## Peer feedback prompts

Use these prompts to help each presenter improve:

1. One thing that is clear and strong.
2. One thing that feels confusing to you.
3. One suggestion to improve visual consistency.
4. One suggestion to improve code structure or implementation approach.

Please be kind :)
